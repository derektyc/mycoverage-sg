Four Good Reasons to Review Your Policies

🛡️ Understand Your Protection

Get a clearer picture of what each policy covers, how much it may pay, and when a claim can be made. Your advisor can walk you through the limits and exclusions that matter to you.

◎ Find Potential Gaps or Overlaps

Look at your policies side by side to see where you may have too little cover or where two plans cover a similar need. That gives you a clearer basis for deciding whether any changes are worth considering.

◇ Review Affordability

Add up what you pay each month or year and see how those premiums fit alongside your other commitments. A review can help you decide what cover to prioritise and what you can comfortably maintain over time.

♡ Keep Up With Life's Changes

Getting married, having children, buying a home or approaching retirement can change what you need from your insurance. A review helps you check whether your current cover still fits the people who depend on you and the commitments you have today.
